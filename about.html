About Angry Grannies

Angry Grannies is the app-making group behind SuDoKu Playtime. The project grew out of the Townsville U3A coding collective, where members learn programming and turn ideas into real software.

How The Group Works

Learn By Making

The collective focuses on practical programming. Members work through ideas step by step and build apps that people can actually use.

Friendly And Accessible

Angry Grannies aims to make puzzle and casual apps that are welcoming, clear, and enjoyable for relaxed play.

Based In Townsville

The Townsville U3A coding collective brings together seniors with an interest in programming, problem solving, and building something useful together.
