About The Team

Angry Grannies grew out of the Townsville U3A coding collective, where seniors learn programming by breaking ideas into small logical steps, practising with Python, and building visible results such as simple applications.

SuDoKu Playtime reflects that spirit: practical learning, creative problem solving, and making something real enough for others to enjoy.
