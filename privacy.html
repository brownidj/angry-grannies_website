Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to SuDoKu Playtime, published by Angry Grannies. The public Privacy Policy URL for App Store Connect is https://angrygrannies.com.au/privacy.html.

Data Collection

SuDoKu Playtime does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide personal information inside the app.

Angry Grannies does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, precise location, or user-generated personal content through SuDoKu Playtime.

Purchases And Third-Party Services

SuDoKu Playtime may use Apple services to process in-app purchases and restore purchases. Apple may process purchase-related information according to Apple's own privacy policies and App Store terms.

SuDoKu Playtime does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Tracking And Linking

Angry Grannies does not sell personal information and does not use SuDoKu Playtime data to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Angry Grannies does not link app usage data to a user identity.

Support Emails

If you contact support by email, Angry Grannies will receive the email address and any information you choose to include, such as your device model, operating system version, app version, and a description of the issue. This information is used only to respond to support requests.

Retention And Deletion

Support emails may be retained for as long as needed to respond to the request, maintain support records, and meet reasonable legal or administrative requirements. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing Angry Grannies.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email support@angrygrannies.com.au.
