# Joshi Navi — App Name and “Coming Soon” Panel Notes

## 1. Recommended app name

A strong name for the Japanese particles app is:

# **Joshi Navi**

This name works well in both English and Japanese.

## 2. Why “Joshi Navi” works

### English use

For English-speaking learners, **Joshi Navi** is short, pronounceable, and distinctive. It suggests a friendly guide for navigating Japanese particles.

The word **Joshi** comes from the Japanese word **助詞**, meaning grammatical particle. These are the small but important words such as:

- は
- が
- を
- に
- で
- へ

The word **Navi** suggests navigation or guidance. Together, **Joshi Navi** communicates the idea of an app that helps learners find their way through Japanese particles.

### Japanese use

In Japanese, the app name can be written as:

# **助詞ナビ**

Reading:

# **じょしナビ / Joshi Navi**

This is clear and natural because:

- **助詞** means grammatical particle.
- **ナビ** is commonly understood in Japanese as “navigation” or “guide.”

So the Japanese meaning is direct: **Particle Guide** or **Particle Navigator**.

## 3. Suggested App Store presentation

For the English-language app listing, a good title would be:

# **Joshi Navi: Japanese Particles**

This keeps the app name memorable while making the purpose clear for people searching the store.

For a Japanese subtitle or store description, a suitable version would be:

# **助詞ナビ：日本語の助詞を楽しく練習**

Meaning:

> Particle Navi: practise Japanese particles enjoyably.
